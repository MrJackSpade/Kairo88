# v1.4.0

The first release of Kairo88, an NEC PC-8801 emulator.

- Plays the PC-8801's disk games and its tape-only games (T88 and CMT).
- A catalog of PC-88 games with descriptions, artwork and each game's machine settings, with every multi-disk set grouped as one game.
- Games that start from a prompt get their startup commands typed for them.
- Your own firmware is imported from a folder you choose.
- Every game's D-pad reaches the arrow keys by default. Mappings follow a cascade: the PC-88 defaults, your all-games mapping, the game's catalog mapping, then your own for the game.
- Scaling anchor: Settings › Graphics pins the picture to any of nine places on the screen, and each game's settings can choose its own.
- On a two-screen device (RG DS), the keyboard, joystick and touchpad are on the bottom screen, and the D-pad moves between the screens in the library.

Both APK variants and the Play bundle have the same features.
