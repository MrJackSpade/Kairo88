# v1.5.3

- The D-pad is the cursor keys alone: it no longer presses the numeric keypad and moves the joystick at the same time. The left stick is the joystick and the right stick the numeric keypad's 8, 4, 6 and 2. X and Y send Z and X alone, and C and Z are the joystick's buttons 1 and 2.
- Every controller button sends one thing per press, in every game's default controls.

Install over v1.5.2 to keep games, settings and saves. Both APK variants and the Play bundle have the same features.
